# 英语 · 场景 1：询盘与开发信

!!! note "这个场景什么时候用"
    两种时刻：①客户主动发来询盘（inquiry），你要回复并收集报价要素；②你主动开发客户（cold email 开发信）。这一场景给你完整的单词、句库、范例和练习——学完即可发出第一封专业邮件。

!!! abstract "本章的三流"
    - 三流视角：询盘邮件是「单据流的第一张单据」——本场景单词几乎都在描述三流要素（数量＝资金规模、drawing＝货物流的定义、surface finish＝货物流的外观工序）。

## 核心单词（12 个）

| 英文 | 中文 | 常见搭配 |
|---|---|---|
| inquiry | 询盘 | receive an inquiry 收到询盘 |
| quote / quotation | 报价 | send a quote 发报价 |
| drawing | 图纸 | share the drawing 发图纸 |
| specification (spec) | 规格 | meet the spec 达到规格 |
| quantity | 数量 | order quantity 订购数量 |
| lead time | 交期 | short lead time 短交期 |
| MOQ | 最小起订量 | low MOQ 低起订量 |
| sample | 样品 | sample fee 样品费 |
| surface finish | 表面处理 | anodized surface 阳极氧化表面 |
| manufacturer | 制造商/工厂 | 有工厂才这样自称 |
| supplier | 供应商 | reliable supplier 可靠供应商 |
| partner workshop | 合作厂 | 协调角色如实措辞 |

## 常见表达句库（按意图分组）

**开场致谢**
- Thanks for your inquiry about the aluminum parts.（感谢你对铝件的询盘）
- We received your inquiry dated March 3.（我们收到了你 3 月 3 日的询盘）

**请求信息（礼貌三件套）**
- Could you share the drawing in STEP or PDF?（能发 STEP 或 PDF 图纸吗）
- Would you mind letting us know the quantity?（方便告知数量吗）
- Please advise the required surface finish.（请告知所需表面处理）

**介绍能力**
- We specialize in precision CNC machining of aluminum parts.（我们专注精密铝件 CNC 加工）
- We have been supplying machined parts to clients in Europe for years.（多年为欧洲客户供货）

**承诺与跟进**
- I'll come back to you with a quote after reviewing the drawing.（看过图纸后发报价）
- Just following up on my email below.（跟进一下之前的邮件）

## 范例一：开发信（cold email，主动出击）

> **Subject: CNC machining partner for your aluminum parts**
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I came across your product page and noticed the machined aluminum parts — nice work. We help hardware brands produce custom CNC machined parts in China: aluminum and stainless steel, from 1-piece prototypes to small batches, with anodizing and sandblasting finishes.
>
> If you ever need a reliable machining partner, feel free to send me a drawing (STEP or PDF) anytime — quotations are free.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]

**拆解**：一句赞美破冰（具体，不空泛）→ 一句能力陈述（材料+起订量+工艺，全是干货）→ 一个零成本的动作引导（发图即报价）。没有「We are the best」，没有长篇公司介绍。

## 范例二：询盘回复（承接 [9.1 报价要素清单](l1-01.md)）

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for your inquiry about the aluminum parts. To quote accurately, could you share:
> - the drawing (STEP or PDF);
> - the quantity for the first order;
> - the required surface finish (e.g., anodized black)?
>
> We arrange precision CNC machining with partner workshops in China, from 1 piece. I'll come back to you with a quote after reviewing the files.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]

## 场景练习

**练习 1 · 填空**：用本课单词补全句子。
1. Thanks for your ______ about the aluminum parts.
2. Could you share the drawing in ______ or PDF?
3. What ______ do you need for the first order?
4. Our ______ is 10 days after the deposit.

**练习 2 · 中译英**：
1. 我们协调中国合作厂提供精密铝件加工，1 件起。
2. 看过图纸后，我把报价发给你。

**练习 3 · 改写（更专业）**：
1. "Send me the drawing now!!"
2. "We are the best factory, cheap price!!"

??? note "参考答案"
    **练习 1**：1. inquiry　2. STEP　3. quantity　4. lead time
    **练习 2**：1. We arrange precision CNC machining of aluminum parts with partner workshops in China, from 1 piece.　2. I'll come back to you with a quote after reviewing the drawing.
    **练习 3**：1. Could you share the drawing when convenient? Thank you.　2. We arrange CNC machining with partner workshops in China — 如实描述角色，不用 "best/cheap" 这类空洞大话。
