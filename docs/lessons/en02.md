# 英语 · 场景 2：报价与议价

!!! note "这个场景什么时候用"
    报价要素收集齐了（[9.1](l1-01.md)），工厂实价回来了——现在要把价格翻译成客户能看懂的英文报价函，并在客户砍价时守住底线、体面让步。

## 核心单词（10 个）

| 英文 | 中文 | 常见搭配 |
|---|---|---|
| unit price | 单价 | unit price for 500pcs |
| validity | 有效期 | The quote is valid for 30 days. |
| discount | 折扣 | volume discount 数量折扣 |
| breakdown | 分项明细 | cost breakdown 成本明细 |
| payment terms | 付款条件 | 30% deposit, 70% before shipment |
| lead time | 交期 | lead time: 15 working days |
| estimate | 估算 | This is an estimate, final quote after DFM. |
| target price | 目标价 | May we know your target price? |
| counter-offer | 还价 | We accept your counter-offer. |
| FOB / DAP | 贸易术语 | quoted on DAP basis |

!!! abstract "本章的三流"
    - 三流视角：报价函是资金流的核心单据；议价句式保护的正是资金流的价格与条款。

## 报价函骨架（范例）

> **Subject: Quotation — Aluminum Bracket (500 pcs)**
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for your patience. Please find our quotation below:
>
> - Material: AL 6061-T6
> - Process: 3-axis CNC machining + black anodizing (Type II)
> - Quantity: 500 pcs
> - Unit price: USD 8.50 (DAP [city])
> - Freight: USD 180, shipping time 7-9 days
> - Payment: 30% deposit, 70% before shipment
> - Lead time: 15 working days after deposit
> - Validity: 30 days
>
> The drawing revision is Rev B, per your confirmation.
>
> If anything needs adjusting — quantity, finish, or tolerance — let me know and I'll re-quote accordingly.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]

**拆解**：价格永远带着「条件」出现（数量、版本、条款、有效期）——这样客户砍价时，每一项条件都是你的谈判筹码。

## 议价句库（按意图分组）

**客户砍价时（让步要交换）**
- At 800 pcs, I can bring the unit price down to USD 7.90.（加量换降价）
- I can offer a 3% discount if we extend the lead time by one week.（价换期）
- The price is already based on our best cost structure — what I can offer instead is a free sample for your next test.（非价格让步）

**礼貌拒绝（守底线不伤人）**
- Unfortunately, below USD 8.00 we would have to compromise on the finish quality, which we don't recommend.
- This is already our best price for this quantity — the next step down would be 1000 pcs.

**报价有效期与刷新**
- The quote is valid until the end of this month — material prices have been moving recently.
- Just a heads-up, the quotation will expire next week. Happy to refresh it if your timing has changed.

**客户比价后（"another supplier quoted lower"）**
- Could you share the specification of that offer? Sometimes quotes look cheaper on different assumptions (finish, tolerance, Incoterms).

## 场景练习

**练习 1 · 填空**：
1. This quote is ______ for 30 days.
2. 30% ______, 70% before shipment.
3. Could we know your ______ price?

**练习 2 · 中译英**：
1. 这个单价是按 DAP 条件报的，含运费。
2. 如果数量到 1000 件，我可以把单价降到 7.9 美元。

**练习 3 · 改写（让步要交换）**：
"I can give you 5% off."（客户什么都没给）

??? success "参考答案"
    **练习 1**：1. valid　2. deposit　3. target
    **练习 2**：1. The unit price is quoted on a DAP basis, freight included.　2. If the quantity reaches 1000 pcs, I can bring the unit price down to USD 7.90.
    **练习 3**：I can offer a 5% discount if we confirm the order this week / if the quantity is 1000 pcs.（任何降价都要换一个条件回来）
