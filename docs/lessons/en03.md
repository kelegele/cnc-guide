# 英语 · 场景 3：订单确认与生产跟进

!!! note "这个场景什么时候用"
    客户说 "OK, please proceed" 之后：确认订单（PI）、收定金、排产、汇报进度——以及最难的「延误告知」。这一场景给你全链路句式。

## 核心单词（10 个）

| 英文 | 中文 | 常见搭配 |
|---|---|---|
| proforma invoice (PI) | 形式发票 | Please find attached the PI. |
| deposit | 定金 | 30% deposit |
| balance | 尾款 | balance before shipment |
| schedule / scheduling | 排产 | production scheduled for… |
| lead time | 交期 | lead time: 15 working days |
| progress | 进度 | progress update 进度更新 |
| ship out / shipment | 出货 | ship out this Friday |
| tracking number | 追踪号 | tracking number attached |
| delay | 延误 | a short delay |
| on schedule | 按计划 | Everything is on schedule. |

## 订单确认函（范例）

> Hi [Name],
>
> Great — confirming your order as below:
>
> - Aluminum bracket, per drawing Rev B
> - Quantity: 500 pcs, black anodized
> - Total: USD 4,430 (DAP [city])
> - Payment: 30% deposit, 70% before shipment
> - Lead time: 15 working days after deposit received
>
> Please find attached the PI for your confirmation. Once the deposit is received, we'll schedule production right away.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]

## 进度汇报（三段式：现在→下一步→预计）

> Hi [Name],
>
> Quick update on your order:
>
> **Now**: Machining is in progress — about 60% of the batch is done.
> **Next**: Surface finishing (black anodizing), then quality inspection.
> **Expected**: On schedule for shipment this Friday; tracking number will follow once shipped.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]

## 延误告知（坏消息早说＋先给方案）

> Hi [Name],
>
> An honest update: the anodizing supplier returned the batch one day later than planned, so shipping will shift from Friday to next Tuesday.
>
> What we're doing: the parts are already packed and will ship the moment they arrive — I'll send photos of the final inspection today so you can see everything is ready.
>
> Sorry for the short delay, and thank you for your understanding.
>
> Best regards,
> [Your name]

**拆解**：先说实话（原因一句带过，不编故事）→ 新日期明确 → 立刻给补救动作（照片先发）→ 简短致歉。**不找借口，先给方案。**

## 场景练习

**练习 1 · 填空**：
1. We'll schedule production once the ______ is received.
2. Everything is on ______ — shipping this Friday.
3. The ______ number will follow once shipped.

**练习 2 · 中译英**：
1. 定金到账后 15 个工作日交货。
2. 加工已完成约 60%，表面处理明天开始。

**练习 3 · 改写（延误告知三要素）**：
"Sorry for the delay, the anodizing factory is slow."

??? success "参考答案"
    **练习 1**：1. deposit　2. schedule　3. tracking
    **练习 2**：1. Delivery: 15 working days after the deposit is received.　2. Machining is about 60% done; surface finishing starts tomorrow.
    **练习 3**：三要素＝原因一句＋新日期＋补救方案。范例：The anodizing supplier needed two extra days, so shipping moves from Friday to Tuesday. The parts are packed and final inspection photos are attached — they'll ship the moment the finish is done.
