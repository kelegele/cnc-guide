# 英语 · 场景 4：品质争议与客诉

!!! note "这个场景什么时候用"
    客户来信说 "The parts don't meet our spec"——最考验英语也最考验情商的场景。这一场景把[热身课坑三](lesson-0.md)的四步结构扩展成完整英文工具箱：受理、调查、方案、复盘。

## 核心单词（10 个）

| 英文 | 中文 | 常见搭配 |
|---|---|---|
| defect | 缺陷 | surface defect 表面缺陷 |
| rework | 返工 | rework the parts at our cost |
| remake / reproduce | 重做 | We'll remake the batch. |
| scrap | 报废 | scrap rate 报废率 |
| inspection report | 检验报告 | inspection report attached |
| dimensional report | 尺寸检测报告 | full dimensional report |
| signed sample | 签样 | per the signed sample |
| concession | 让步接收 | accept as a concession |
| root cause | 根本原因 | root cause analysis |
| corrective action | 纠正措施 | corrective action plan |

## 四步结构英文化（受理→调查→方案→复盘）

### 第一步：受理（回应快，不辩解）

> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for the detailed feedback and photos — we take this seriously and are looking into it right away.

### 第二步：调查（摆证据，不甩锅）

> We've compared the parts against the signed sample and the drawing (Rev B), and had our QC run a full dimensional check.
>
> Two quick questions to make sure we're aligned:
> 1. Which dimension is out of tolerance on your side?
> 2. Could you confirm the gauge you used for the measurement?

### 第三步：方案（三选一给客户）

> Based on our findings, here's what we can do:
> 1. **Rework**: we re-machine the affected surfaces at our cost;
> 2. **Remake**: we remake the batch and ship within X days;
> 3. **Concession**: if the parts still function for your application, we'd like to offer a X% credit on this batch.
>
> Whichever you prefer — your call.

### 第四步：复盘（重建信任）

> To prevent this from happening again, we've added an extra in-process check on [the affected dimension] before packing, and updated our QC checklist.

## 定责前后的措辞差异

| 情况 | 该说 | 不该说 |
|---|---|---|
| 事实未明 | We're checking on our side. | That's not our problem. |
| 我方责任 | You're right — the parts are out of tolerance. We'll remake at our cost, including shipping. | 装看不见 |
| 客户误判 | The drawing we have (Rev B) shows ±0.05 here — could you confirm which revision you're checking against? | 你搞错了 |

## 场景练习

**练习 1 · 填空**：
1. We've compared the parts against the ______ sample.
2. We can ______ the affected surfaces at our cost.
3. Could you accept these parts as a ______ with a 10% credit?

**练习 2 · 中译英**：
1. 尺寸检测报告已附上，供你核查。
2. 我们会分析根本原因，并在下一批增加一道过程检验。

**练习 3 · 改写**：
"It's your gauge that's wrong. Our parts are fine."

??? success "参考答案"
    **练习 1**：1. signed　2. rework　3. concession
    **练习 2**：1. The dimensional report is attached for your review.　2. We'll analyze the root cause and add an extra in-process inspection for the next batch.
    **练习 3**：There may be a measurement difference between our gauges. Could you confirm the gauge type and the drawing revision you're checking against? Happy to run a joint comparison on the next parts.
